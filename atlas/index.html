Who does what in AI, from power to people.

AI runs on a chain of twelve links, from the power that feeds a data centre to the people who use what it produces. Each link has a core business and four layers around it: what it depends on, what makes it faster or cheaper, what keeps it safe, and how it reaches buyers. Everything here is sourced, open, and corrected in public.

Type a company or pick what you are. You will see only what matters to you: who you sell to and what you offer them, your peers, your partners and marketplaces, what you build on, and the investors active in your space.

How it works
  1. Use “Suggest” on any box or listing. It opens a short form on GitHub.
  2. Companies can claim their listing from their organisation's GitHub account to keep it accurate.
  3. Developers can edit the node file directly in a pull request; an automatic check validates it.
  4. Fork the repository to run your own version. The data is CC BY 4.0: credit Progma and link back.
