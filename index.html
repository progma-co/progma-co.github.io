The outcome, and what it takes

What you get, and what we actually do.

For startups

GTM, growth and scale

Outcome: A mutually agreed revenue realisation or active customer target, owned and achieved within a timeline. The replicable roadmap — with the process and people to run it — in place to carry on and scale.

01

GTM

Right ICPs; relevant channels; pricing discovery; product-led, sales-led, or marketing-led motions — and the right hand-offs between them; the right pipeline to track. Do, learn, iterate — get it across the line.

02

Process setup and tool implementation

Setting up the execution rigor across product and engineering, sales, marketing, and customer success. Identifying and setting up the right tooling to enable that rigor.

03

Data and dashboards

Visibility, the right interpretations, the actions that follow, and the review cadence around all of it.

04

People

Which roles are required — defining them, finding them, integrating them, and setting them up to run this, long after Progma is out of the picture.

For SMBs, GCCs and enterprises

Operating model for transformation

Outcome: A redesigned operating model that aligns to the transformation. The governance rhythm in place to run on its own, without Progma in the room.

01

Strategy

Right prioritization and the right measures — what it's solving, and what needle it moves.

02

Process and operating model

The breakdown and flow of information, context, and ownership across the organization.

03

Org design

Who does what — the hierarchies that hold, the ones that crunch, cross-functional interface streamlining, and manpower plans.

04

Cadences

The review framework that ensures the changes stick.

For CHROs and people leaders

Talent model: aligning performance and skill

Outcome: A capability and performance model agreed and adopted within a timeline — role definitions, a skill model, and a performance framework your people actually run against. Managers already fluent in running it, without us in the room.

01

Role and skill mapping

A clear picture of which roles, skills, and levels are out of date, and by how much. We do whatever it takes to map it — role by role, function by function.

02

Role and capability redesign

Role definitions and a skill model that actually match the work now. We do whatever it takes, built with the managers who'll use it.

03

Performance framework

Performance reviews that reward the work that actually matters now. We do whatever it takes — piloted with one function before it goes org-wide.

04

Manager and team enablement

Managers who are fluent in the new roles, skills, and performance bar before we leave. We do whatever it takes to get them there.
